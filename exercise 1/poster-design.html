Poster Design

This is one of my creative design projects. I created a promotional poster using Adobe Photoshop.

Project Description

This project is a promotional poster featuring the Nissan GT-R, one of the most iconic Japanese sports car. The main purpose of this poster is to showcase the car's powerful performance, attractive appearance and unique identity.

Design Concept

I chose black and red color scheme to create a bold, powerful and agressive theme. I combined several Nissan GT-R images to highlight different angles and details of the car. The large Nissan typography and the slogan "The Godzilla of the Streets" helps create a strong visual impact.

Design Process

I started by selecting suitable Nissan Gt-R images and arranging them into a collage layout. Next, I chose red and black background to match the automotive theme. I then added the title, slogan and vechile specifications to make the poster more informative and visually attractive.

What I Learned

Through this project, I improved my skills in image arrangement, typography, color selection and poster composition. I also learned how to comhine images and text to create an eye-catching promotional design.
